# Empathy Scale

## Purpose
This scale measures the human worker’s perception of the musculoskeletal robot’s empathetic behavior during close, shared‑space assembly collaboration. Items assess the robot’s ability to communicate intent, its spatial yielding, its respect for the human’s comfort, and its avoidance of blocking or contact.

## Items
Respond to each statement on a 5‑point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Agree, 5 = Strongly Agree).

1. The robot clearly indicated when it was about to reach for a tool or part.  
2. The robot verbally signaled its intentions (e.g., “我来拿这个” or “你先拿”) in a way that helped me anticipate its movements.  
3. When I reached for an item, the robot paused or yielded to let me take it first.  
4. The robot changed its arm path or retracted its arm when it saw my hand approaching the same area.  
5. The robot left enough space for me at the shared tool rack without crowding my side of the workstation.  
6. The robot’s proximity to my upper body felt comfortable, similar to working with another human.  
7. The robot avoided blocking my reach during the task.  
8. The robot sometimes reached for the same item I was reaching for, causing a collision or delay. (R)  
9. When accidental contact occurred, the robot immediately paused and did not continue moving against me.  
10. The robot did not give any warning before its arm moved into my personal space. (R)  
11. The robot’s movements felt considerate of where I was positioned and what I was doing.  
12. Overall, the robot seemed aware of my presence and adjusted its actions to avoid interfering with me.

*(R) denotes reverse‑scored items.*

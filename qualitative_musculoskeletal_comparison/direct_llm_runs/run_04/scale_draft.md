# Empathy Scale

## Purpose
This scale assesses the human collaborator's perception of empathic behavior exhibited by a musculoskeletal robot during a shared assembly task. Empathy in this context refers to the robot's ability to communicate intent, yield space, respect personal boundaries, and adapt its movements to avoid collisions or discomfort, thereby fostering a cooperative and safe interaction akin to human–human collaboration.

## Items
*For each statement, indicate your level of agreement on a 5‑point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Agree, 5 = Strongly Agree).*

1. The robot clearly indicated its intended reach before moving toward the shared tool rack.
2. When we both reached for the same part, the robot yielded or gave way to me.
3. The robot left enough space for me to comfortably access the tool rack.
4. The robot adjusted its arm trajectory to avoid coming too close to my body.
5. The robot’s verbal phrases (e.g., “我来拿这个” or “你先拿”) helped me understand its next action.
6. I felt that the robot was considerate of my personal space during the task.
7. The robot’s arm movements were smooth and accommodating rather than abrupt or intrusive.
8. The robot sometimes blocked my intended reach without any warning or adjustment. (R)
9. I experienced moments of unintended physical contact with the robot that the robot did not attempt to avoid. (R)
10. When I needed to reach into an area the robot was using, it responded appropriately by pausing or moving aside.
11. The robot’s pauses, retractions, or path changes showed awareness of my simultaneous movements.
12. I felt the robot treated me as a collaborator sharing the same workspace, not just as an obstacle to avoid.
13. The robot’s behavior made me feel safe and comfortable working side by side with it.
14. The robot seemed to anticipate my reaching motions and adjusted its own actions accordingly.
15. Overall, I perceived the robot as demonstrating empathic understanding of my needs during the collaboration.

*(R) denotes reverse‑scored items.*

# 10个机器人共情的语义方面

基于PETS、RoPE和Putta等论文，以下是评估机器人共情时需要覆盖的10个不同语义方面：

## 1. 认知理解 (Cognitive Understanding)
**定义**: 系统理解用户的需求、目标、意图和内部状态的能力。
**参考**: PETS-UT (Understanding and Trust), RoPE Empathic Understanding
**示例条目**:
- "The system understood my goals."
- "The system understood my needs."
- "The robot knows me and my needs."

## 2. 情绪响应 (Emotional Responsiveness)
**定义**: 系统识别、处理和响应用户情绪状态的能力，包括情绪识别和情绪表达。
**参考**: PETS-ER (Emotional Responsiveness), Putta论文中的emotional communication
**示例条目**:
- "The system considered my mental state."
- "The system seemed emotionally intelligent."
- "The system expressed emotions."

## 3. 支持行为 (Supportive Behavior)
**定义**: 系统提供帮助、鼓励、安慰等支持性行为的能力。
**参考**: RoPE Empathic Response, Putta论文中的compassionate empathy
**示例条目**:
- "The system supported me in coping with an emotional situation."
- "The robot comforts me when I am upset."
- "The robot helps me when I need it."

## 4. 视角采择 (Perspective-Taking)
**定义**: 系统从用户的角度理解和感知事物的能力。
**参考**: Putta论文中的cognitive processes (perspective-taking), PETS中的understanding
**示例条目**:
- "The robot appreciates exactly how the things I experience feel to me."
- "The system understood my intentions."

## 5. 信任安全 (Trust and Safety)
**定义**: 系统建立用户信任、确保安全、尊重用户的能力。
**参考**: PETS-UT (Understanding and Trust), Putta论文中的Empathic Relationship
**示例条目**:
- "I trusted the system."
- "The artificial agent/robot respects me."
- "The robot prioritizes my safety over task completion."

## 6. 沟通质量 (Communication Quality)
**定义**: 系统沟通的清晰度、自然度、及时性和有效性。
**参考**: Putta论文中的emotional communication (emotion expression), RoPE中的communication
**示例条目**:
- "The robot communicates safety protocols effectively."
- "The artificial agent/robot always gives me an immediate response."

## 7. 适应性 (Adaptability)
**定义**: 系统根据用户状态、需求或情境调整其行为的能力。
**参考**: Putta论文中提到的adapt behavior, RoPE中的adaptive response
**示例条目**:
- "The robot alters its tasks based on my performance."
- "The robot matches its rhythm to my work pace."

## 8. 主动意识 (Proactive Awareness)
**定义**: 系统主动识别用户需求、预测用户意图、提前采取行动的能力。
**参考**: Putta论文中的prediction, PETS中的showing interest
**示例条目**:
- "The system showed interest in me."
- "The robot anticipates my needs before I express them."

## 9. 错误处理 (Error Handling)
**定义**: 系统处理错误、失败情况、用户挫折时的共情反应能力。
**参考**: 各论文中隐含的错误容忍和恢复能力
**示例条目**:
- "The robot acknowledges my mistakes without judgment."
- "The system adapts when I make errors."

## 10. 情境敏感性 (Contextual Sensitivity)
**定义**: 系统理解当前情境、环境因素、任务上下文并据此调整行为的能力。
**参考**: Putta论文中的contextual understanding, PETS中的scenario-specific empathy
**示例条目**:
- "The robot adjusts its behavior based on the current situation."
- "The system understands the context of our interaction."

## 参考文献

1. **PETS (Schmidmaier et al., 2024)**: 
   - Emotional Responsiveness (ER): 6 items
   - Understanding and Trust (UT): 4 items

2. **RoPE (Charrier et al., 2019)**:
   - Empathic Understanding (EU): 8 items
   - Empathic Response (ER): 8 items

3. **Putta et al. (2022)**:
   - Empathic Understanding
   - Empathic Response
   - Empathic Relationship
   - Emotional communication (recognition & expression)
   - Emotion regulation
   - Cognitive processes (appraisal, re-appraisal, perspective-taking)

## 使用建议

在生成条目时，应确保：
1. 每个维度都有条目覆盖
2. 条目测量不同的语义方面，而非仅措辞不同
3. 条目与场景相关（robot platform, interaction modalities, collaboration pattern, environmental setting）
4. 条目能够区分不同体验的参与者（有足够方差）

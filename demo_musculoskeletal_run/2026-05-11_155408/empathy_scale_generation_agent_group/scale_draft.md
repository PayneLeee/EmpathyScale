# Empathy Scale (Draft)

## Purpose and Context
This empathy scale is designed to assess perceived empathy in the context: Human and musculoskeletal robot sit side by side at an assembly workstation, sharing a tool rack. Human does fine manipulation requiring judgment; robot handles heavier or closer items. Timing/coordination errors can lead to simultaneous reaching, blocking, and minor physical contact or collision..

## Structure
- **Dimensions/Subscales**: **Spatial Coordination Understanding**, **Collision-Responsive Adaptation**, **Explicit Communication Repair**
- **Response Format**: 5-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)
- **Administration Notes**: Participants rate items based on their interaction experience.

## Items by Dimension

### Spatial Coordination Understanding
- Item 1: The robot paused its arm to let me reach first.
- Item 2: I could tell the robot was aware of my working area.
- Item 3: The robot changed its path to avoid reaching at the same time as me.
- Item 4: The robot left enough space for me to access the tool rack.
- Item 5: The robot's movements showed it understood where I needed to reach.
- Item 6: I felt the robot respected my personal space at the workstation.
- Item 7: The robot yielded when I started to reach for a part.
- Item 8: The robot's arm trajectory made it easy to anticipate its next move.
- Item 9: The robot adjusted its reach to avoid blocking my hand.
- Item 10: I could predict when the robot would reach for a tool by watching its arm.
- Item 11: The robot seemed to know when I needed a particular tool.
- Item 12: The robot's pauses helped us coordinate who takes which part.

### Collision-Responsive Adaptation
- Item 13: When we bumped, the robot immediately retracted its arm.
- Item 14: The robot apologized verbally after accidental contact.
- Item 15: I felt the robot cared about my safety when it stopped after touching me.
- Item 16: The robot's quick reaction to contact reassured me.
- Item 17: After a collision, the robot adjusted its movement to avoid repeating it.
- Item 18: The robot seemed sensitive to physical contact with me.
- Item 19: I felt the robot was considerate when it pulled back after a bump.
- Item 20: The robot's response to accidental touch made me feel respected.
- Item 21: When our arms touched, the robot paused and then moved more carefully.
- Item 22: The robot's behavior after contact showed it understood my discomfort.
- Item 23: I trusted the robot more because of how it handled unintended contact.
- Item 24: The robot's immediate retraction after a bump felt protective.

### Explicit Communication Repair
- Item 25: When we both reached at the same time, the robot said '你先拿' to let me go first.
- Item 26: The robot's verbal phrases helped resolve coordination conflicts.
- Item 27: I found the robot's spoken cues clear when we needed to take turns.
- Item 28: The robot's words ('我来拿这个') made its intention obvious.
- Item 29: After a misreach, the robot's verbal response quickly got us back on track.
- Item 30: The robot's short phrases reduced my frustration during simultaneous reaching.
- Item 31: I felt the robot communicated effectively when we almost collided.
- Item 32: The robot's verbal repair statements restored smooth collaboration.
- Item 33: When coordination failed, the robot's words helped me understand what to do next.
- Item 34: The robot's spoken instructions made the shared workspace feel safer.
- Item 35: I appreciated that the robot verbally indicated when it would wait for me.
- Item 36: The robot's use of phrases like '你先拿' showed it understood my need to reach.

## Scoring
Items will be scored on a Likert scale from 1 to 5. Subscale totals can be calculated by summing items within each dimension.

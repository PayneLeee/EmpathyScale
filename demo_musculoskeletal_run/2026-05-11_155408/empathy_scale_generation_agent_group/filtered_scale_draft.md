# Empathy Scale (Filtered)

## Items by Factor (EFA-based)

*Note: Items are organized by EFA factors (n=2). Factor structure determined by statistical analysis.*

### Coordination Communication (6 items)
- Item 1: The robot's arm trajectory made it easy to anticipate its next move.
- Item 2: I could predict when the robot would reach for a tool by watching its arm.
- Item 3: When we both reached at the same time, the robot said '你先拿' to let me go first.
- Item 4: The robot's words ('我来拿这个') made its intention obvious.
- Item 5: After a misreach, the robot's verbal response quickly got us back on track.
- Item 6: I felt the robot communicated effectively when we almost collided.

### Shared Workspace Consideration (4 items)
- Item 7: The robot's movements showed it understood where I needed to reach.
- Item 8: The robot's short phrases reduced my frustration during simultaneous reaching.
- Item 9: The robot's spoken instructions made the shared workspace feel safer.
- Item 10: I appreciated that the robot verbally indicated when it would wait for me.
